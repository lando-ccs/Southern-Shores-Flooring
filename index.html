What we install

Pick the floor. We handle everything from removal to the last piece of trim.

Most popular

Luxury vinyl plank (LVP)

Looks like wood, shrugs off water, sand and pets. The go-to for beach homes, condos and rentals because it's tough, quiet underfoot and easy to keep clean.

Laminate

A wood look on a tighter budget, with a hard surface that resists scratches.

Glue-down wood

Real wood bonded right to the slab. Solid feel, no hollow sound.

Tile

Floors, bathrooms, shower walls and kitchen backsplashes, set flat and grouted clean.

Trim that finishes it

Baseboards, quarter round, transitions and stair nosing, done as part of your floor job. No second contractor.
